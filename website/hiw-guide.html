Understanding HIW dental inspections

Healthcare Inspectorate Wales (HIW) — Arolygiaeth Gofal Iechyd Cymru (AGIC) in Welsh — is the independent regulator for all private and NHS-facing dental practices in Wales. Here's what every practice needs to know, in plain English and Welsh.

What's actually inspected

Four frameworks, one inspection

Practices must satisfy several overlapping regulatory frameworks at once, with no single system tying evidence back to each requirement — until now.

HIW registration conditions

The core conditions every registered practice must meet on an ongoing basis, not just at inspection time.

IR(ME)R 2017

Ionising Radiation (Medical Exposure) Regulations — governs safe use of dental X-ray equipment.

WHTM 01-05

Welsh Health Technical Memorandum for decontamination — sterilisation and infection control standards.

GDC Standards

General Dental Council Standards for the Dental Team — professional conduct and patient care standards.

Where practices actually fall down

It's rarely clinical care

HIW's own published inspection insights show recurring failures cluster around non-clinical, avoidable areas:

  • Missing or incomplete reference checks for new staff
  • Incomplete employment history verification
  • Missing DBS (Disclosure and Barring Service) checks
  • Poor evidence organisation, making it hard to demonstrate compliance quickly during a visit
  • Expired certificates and training that went unnoticed until inspection week
Why a Wales-specific system

HIW is not CQC — and off-the-shelf tools show it

Every established competitor in this space is built around CQC (the England regulator). None map to HIW's regulatory language, evidence categories or inspection format — and none offer a genuine multi-practice Welsh group view.

Security & data protection

Built to handle sensitive practice data properly

Practices handle staff DBS checks, health records and other sensitive personal data. That's a baseline requirement we design around, not an afterthought.

UK GDPR & data residency

Full UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018 compliance, with data held in the UK. Group Admin access to roll-up data is itself justified and audited.

Strong authentication

Multi-factor authentication as a minimum for Principal and Group Admin accounts, with granular, time-boxed access grants for inspectors.

Full audit trail

Every view, edit and upload is logged — for every practice, and for every group roll-up view.

Accessibility is built to a WCAG 2.1 AA baseline, and the platform is designed for the availability and support responsiveness practices need most during the 12-week inspection window.
